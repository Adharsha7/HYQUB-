About

Next-Generation Security

HYQUB is a payment wallet that protects each transaction with a post-quantum ML-DSA signature. Every payment is verified by two independent verifiers before the smart wallet executes it.

How it works

01

Sign up

Create your account and initialize your local post-quantum wallet.

02

Key setup

Generate your ML-DSA-65 public-private keypair securely on your device.

03

Create intent

Specify the recipient and payment amount to build a transaction intent.

04

Sign with ML-DSA

Authorize the transfer using a quantum-resistant lattice-based signature.

05

Verify and submit

Two independent verifiers validate the quorum before on-chain execution.

Architecture

Security by Design

Post-quantum signatures

Protected by ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204), securing authentication against future quantum computing threats.

2-of-2 verification

Independent dual-verifier quorum validates signatures and transaction parameters before contract execution.

Smart wallet

Account abstraction (ERC-4337 style) ensures programmable execution, daily limits, and security rules.
